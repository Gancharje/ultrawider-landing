Popup didn't appear?
  1. Make sure the video is actually fullscreen (press the ⛶ button in the player, or double-click the video).
  2. Move your mouse — the popup hides itself after a few seconds and comes back on movement.
  3. Check the extension is enabled: puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar → Ultrawider should be listed.
  4. Reload the video tab once after installing — tabs opened before the install don't have Ultrawider in them yet.
  5. Still nothing? Email hello@ultrawider.net — a human answers.
