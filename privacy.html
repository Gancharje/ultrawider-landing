Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 July 2026 · Effective: 5 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Akimov Pavel Andreevich, a self-employed individual registered in the Russian Federation (INN 482424418541), Lipetsk ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Ultrawider browser extension and the ultrawider.net website (together, the "Service"). We have designed the Service to collect the absolute minimum required to deliver and bill for it.

1. Summary

  • The extension processes video locally inside your browser. We do not see, log, or transmit what you are watching.
  • For Alpha Access we store only your email address and the fact that you hold an active licence.
  • Card and banking details are handled exclusively by our payment aggregator Prodamus. We never see or store them.
  • No advertising, no behavioural profiling, no third-party tracking.
  • You have rights under GDPR, the Russian personal-data law (152-ФЗ), and analogous laws elsewhere — including access, correction, and deletion.

2. What we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Email address — for licence delivery, receipts, licence recovery, and required service notices.
  • Payment metadata — transaction ID, amount, currency, and timestamp returned to us by Prodamus. We do not receive card numbers, CVCs, or bank account numbers.
  • Support correspondence — if you email us, we keep that conversation in order to respond and to improve the Service.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Anonymous installation count — provided by the Chrome Web Store statistics dashboard. Not tied to your identity.
  • Crash and error reports — when the extension or the website encounters an unhandled JavaScript error, we automatically send a technical report containing: the error message and stack trace, the page hostname only (e.g. youtube.com — never the full URL, path, or query string), the browser/OS string, and an anonymous install ID (a random UUID generated at first install, not linked to your email or any other identifier). Crash reports are always sent regardless of the telemetry toggle, because we need this signal to keep the product working. They contain no URLs, no PII, no video content, no credentials, and no licence key.
  • Behavioural telemetry (extension only) — when the extension loads on a page, when you open a video in fullscreen with our HUD visible, and when you select a viewing mode, the extension sends an anonymous event containing: your install ID, the page hostname (e.g. youtube.com — NOT the URL or video ID), your monitor's aspect-ratio tier (16:9, 21:9, 24:9, or 32:9), and screen dimensions. We use this to understand which sites our users actually want ultrawide support on and to find compatibility gaps.

    This is OFF by default — behavioural telemetry is strictly opt-in starting with v1.0.1. You can turn it on or off at any time by opening the Ultrawider popup (toolbar icon) and toggling «Анонимная телеметрия» / "Anonymous telemetry". The toggle controls only these usage-analytics events; the anonymous crash and error reports described above are always sent (hostname only, no URLs) because we need them to keep the product working. We send no behavioural events unless you opt in.
  • Anonymous website analytics — the ultrawider.net website (NOT the extension) loads Yandex.Metrica to measure page views, scroll depth, and clicks so we can improve the landing experience. Metrica may set cookies and record a session replay ("Webvisor"). It does not link back to your licence, your email, or anything you do inside the extension. You can block it with any tracker-blocker, with your browser's "Do Not Track" header, or by visiting the Yandex.Metrica opt-out page. The Chrome extension itself ships no analytics — Chrome Web Store policy forbids it.
  • Server logs — when you visit ultrawider.net or hit our licence-check endpoint, our hosting provider may log standard request metadata (IP, user-agent, timestamp) for security and abuse prevention. Kept for no more than 30 days.

2.3 What we do NOT collect

  • Your browsing history.
  • The contents of pages you visit.
  • Frames, audio, transcripts, or any other data extracted from videos you watch.
  • Personal viewing history (which video you watched, when, where).
  • Card numbers, CVCs, or bank account details.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • deliver your Alpha Access licence and provide ongoing service;
  • process your purchase, issue receipts, and validate your licence;
  • send you required service messages (refund confirmations, security notices, material changes to these terms);
  • respond to your support requests;
  • detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents;
  • comply with applicable law.

We do not use your personal data to train AI models, to build advertising profiles, or to sell to third parties.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

For users in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, we process personal data on the following bases:

  • Contract — to deliver Alpha Access you have purchased (email, licence records, payment metadata).
  • Legitimate interests — minimal anonymous analytics, abuse prevention, security logging.
  • Legal obligation — to keep records required by tax or consumer-protection law.
  • Consent — where required, for example for optional non-essential cookies, with the right to withdraw at any time.

5. Third parties

We rely on a small number of vendors:

  • Prodamus / Tazapay — payment aggregation and processing.
  • Google Chrome Web Store — distribution of the extension. Subject to Google's policies.
  • Hosting provider (Fly.io) — serves this website and the licence-check endpoint.
  • Yandex.Metrica (LLC «YANDEX», Russia) — anonymous analytics on the website only. Privacy notice.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (licence emails, recovery emails).

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

ultrawider.net uses:

  • One first-party cookie (ultrawider_lang) to remember your language preference (EN/RU) between visits.
  • Yandex.Metrica cookies (_ym_uid, _ym_d, _ym_isad, _ym_visorc) — anonymous analytics described in §2.2 above. Lifetime: up to 1 year.

We do not use advertising pixels, retargeting pixels, or any cross-context behavioural advertising. The browser extension itself does not set cookies.

7. International transfers

Personal data may be processed in the Russian Federation, the EEA, or other jurisdictions where our vendors operate. Where personal data is transferred from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or another legally recognised transfer mechanism.

8. Data retention

  • Licence and email records: kept while your licence is active, deleted within 30 days of a verified deletion request (or anonymised for tax/accounting purposes if longer retention is required by law).
  • Payment metadata: kept for the period required by Russian tax and accounting law.
  • Support emails: kept up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Server logs: kept up to 30 days, then deleted.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate data;
  • request deletion ("right to be forgotten");
  • request a portable copy of your data;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@ultrawider.net from the address on your licence. We respond within 30 days.

10. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards including HTTPS, encryption at rest where supported by our hosting provider, restricted access to production systems, and the principle of data minimisation.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent change. For material changes we will make reasonable efforts to notify users by email or via the extension.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise any of the rights described above, contact us at hello@ultrawider.net.
