Refund Policy

Last updated: 20 May 2026 · Effective: 20 May 2026

Также доступно на русском: Политика возвратов

We want you to be confident in your purchase. If Ultrawider doesn't work for you, we will refund you in full within the windows described below — no questions asked.

1. 14-day money-back guarantee

Every Alpha Access purchase comes with a 14-day full or partial refund window, starting from the date your payment is processed by Prodamus. Within those 14 days you can request a refund for any reason, or no reason at all. This 14-day period aligns with EU consumer-protection law (Directive 2011/83/EU).

2. What is covered

  • Full refund of the Alpha Access payment (USD 3.99) within 14 days of the original purchase date.
  • Partial refund available on request (for example, if you experienced limited use of the product before requesting cancellation).

3. What is not covered

  • Refund requests submitted more than 14 days after the original transaction date, except in cases of unresolved technical defects (see section 5).
  • Refund requests where there is clear evidence of fraud or abuse (for example, repeated purchase-and-refund cycles).

None of the above limits any non-waivable statutory right you may have as a consumer under the laws of your country of residence.

4. How to request a refund

Send an email to hello@ultrawider.net with the subject line "Refund Request". Please include:

  • the email address you used at checkout;
  • your order ID (from the Prodamus confirmation email);
  • the approximate date of purchase, if known.

We confirm receipt within 1 business day and process qualifying refunds within 7 to 14 business days back to the original payment method via Prodamus. We do not need to know why you are requesting a refund.

5. After 14 days — technical defects

Outside the 14-day window we evaluate refund requests on a case-by-case basis, especially where a technical defect prevents you from using the product. We will work with you in good faith to resolve any issue and issue a refund where a fix is not feasible.

6. Processing times and currency

Refunds are processed in the same currency and to the same payment method as the original transaction, via Prodamus. Depending on your payment provider, the funds may take additional days to appear on your statement after we issue them. Any currency-conversion differences between the date of purchase and the date of refund are determined by your payment provider and are not within our control.

7. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is incorrect, please email us first — we almost always resolve issues faster than a bank or card-network chargeback. Initiating a chargeback before contacting us may delay resolution and may, in cases of repeated abuse, result in suspension of the affected licence.

8. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits any statutory right of withdrawal, cancellation, or refund that you have as a consumer under the mandatory laws of your country of residence. Where local law grants a more generous right than this policy, that local law prevails to the extent of the conflict. Russian customers are also protected by Russian consumer-protection law (Закон РФ "О защите прав потребителей", ст. 26.1).

9. Contact

All refund requests and related questions go to hello@ultrawider.net.
