Netflix on ultrawide: killing the black bars

Why DRM makes this harder, and how Ultrawider works around it.

Netflix streams in 16:9. Your monitor is 21:9. You get two thick black bars. Netflix has no "stretch" or "fill" setting. Here's what works and what doesn't.

Why most solutions fail on Netflix

Netflix uses DRM (Widevine/PlayReady) to protect content. Traditional video-manipulation extensions use canvas overlays — they draw the video to a hidden canvas, process pixels, then display the result. But DRM content is blackboxed: when you try to read pixels from a DRM video, you get a black rectangle. The browser literally prevents JavaScript from seeing the frame.

This is why most "video stretcher" extensions work on YouTube but show a black screen on Netflix.

How Ultrawider is different

Ultrawider doesn't read pixels. Instead, it applies an SVG filter with a displacement map directly to the video element. The browser's GPU compositor executes the filter — the DRM pipeline renders the frame normally, then the compositor warps it. No pixel access needed. No DRM bypassed.

The result: Smoothie warp works on Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Twitch, Crunchyroll and every other DRM-protected platform.

What it looks like

✅ Black bars disappear — the full frame fills your 21:9 screen
✅ Faces stay true (non-linear warp keeps center undistorted)
✅ Subtitles stay readable (positioned below the warp zone)
✅ No performance hit (GPU-composited, zero CPU cost)
✅ No DRM warnings or playback errors

How to set it up

1. Install Ultrawider from the Chrome Web Store or Microsoft Store
2. Open Netflix and play any show
3. Press F for fullscreen — Ultrawider's mini-panel appears
4. Choose Smoothie (warp) or Crop (zoom)
5. Done. It remembers your preference per site.

Troubleshooting

Black screen? Make sure hardware acceleration is enabled in your browser settings. The SVG filter needs GPU compositing.

Quality looks soft? If Netflix is streaming at low bitrate, stretching amplifies compression. Check the quality indicator in the status bar — if it says "soft source", switch to Crop mode.
