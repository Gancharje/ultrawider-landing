Where UltraWideo wins

Best store rating (4.4★), 70K users, 41 languages, cross-browser (Chrome + Firefox). Their letterbox detection crops bars baked into the video itself (not just player letterboxing) — clever. Per-path rules let you configure individual channels/videos differently. Cloud sync. Ambient light effect.

Where Ultrawider wins

1. Smoothie warp: UltraWideo has stretch modes but no non-linear stretching — uniform stretch only. Faces look 34% wider. Our Smoothie preserves center at exactly 1.0× — faces stay true.

2. Quality recommendations: We measure stream quality and recommend Crop when Smoothie would look soft. Unique.

3. Price + trial: $24/yr flat with 7-day full trial. No locked PRO features behind freemium gates during trial.

4. Simpler: Two modes instead of four. Quality recommendations mean you don't have to figure out which mode to use — we tell you.

Bottom line

UltraWideo is the most polished competitor — great rating, broad platform support, rich features. But they don't have non-linear stretching, which is the core of what ultrawide video should look like. If faces looking natural matters to you, that's the difference.
