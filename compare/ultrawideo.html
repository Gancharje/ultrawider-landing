Why people choose Ultrawider

Faces stay natural

UltraWideo has four stretch modes, but none are non-linear — every pixel stretches equally. On a 21:9 screen that's 34% wider faces. Smoothie stretches edges far more than center, so faces at screen center look exactly right.

Two modes, not four

UltraWideo's Normal / Upscale / Stretch / Custom is powerful but requires you to figure out which to use. Ultrawider has Smoothie and Crop — and quality recommendations tell you which fits your current stream.

Flat $24/yr, full trial

7-day full Pro trial with no card, then $24/year. No freemium gates during the trial — you experience everything before deciding.

Simpler to use

One click to switch modes via mini-switcher. No settings panel, no mode manager, no learning curve. Install, fullscreen, done.

When UltraWideo might work better: if you're on Firefox, need 41 languages, or want ambient light and per-URL rules. Their letterbox detection (cropping bars baked into the video) is also clever. Genuinely well-built — but no non-linear stretching.
