Where Zoom to Fill wins

They're bigger (200K vs our ~600), more mature, and have a GPU effects suite we don't: frame interpolation (Fluidity), HDR Boost, skin-aware Vibrance, halo-free Sharpness. If you want video enhancement beyond stretching — they offer more knobs.

Where Ultrawider wins

1. Price: $24/yr vs $35.99/yr — 33% cheaper.

2. Try before you pay: Full 7-day Pro trial with every feature unlocked, no credit card. Zoom to Fill locks Elastic Stretch (their equivalent of Smoothie) behind the paywall — you can't try non-linear stretching before paying.

3. Smarter recommendations: Ultrawider measures your stream quality in real time. If the bitrate is too low for stretching, it tells you to use Crop instead — Smoothie on a bad source looks soft, and we'd rather you know. No other extension does this.

4. Faster switching: One click from anywhere in fullscreen via our mini-switcher. No panel to open, no settings to navigate.

5. Center-exact warp: Our Smoothie has mathematically zero distortion at screen center (1.0× stretch ratio) with a log-linear curve to the edges. Faces stay true.

Bottom line

If you want GPU video enhancement (interpolation, HDR, vibrance) and don't mind paying $36/yr → Zoom to Fill.

If you want to fill your ultrawide screen with natural-looking video, get told which mode fits your source quality, switch in one click, and pay less → Ultrawider.

Try both, keep the better one: 7-day free trial → (no card) or install free (YouTube forever)
