Where Ultrawidify wins

Free and open source. No paywall, no trial, no email — install and use forever. MIT licensed, auditable code, community-driven. If you want free and don't need non-linear stretching, it's a solid choice.

Firefox support. Works on Chrome, Firefox (including Android). We don't support Firefox.

5 years of community trust. Grew organically through r/ultrawidemasterrace and GitHub.

Where Ultrawider wins

1. Faces look natural. Ultrawidify stretches uniformly — everyone looks 34% wider. Our Smoothie warp preserves center at 1.0× — faces stay true.

2. Reliability: Their store rating is 2.8★ — many users report bugs and breakage on streaming sites. We specifically optimize for Netflix, Prime, Disney+ with engine-truth verification.

3. Smarter: Quality recommendations, mini-switcher, 7-day full trial, per-site preferences with auto-restore across sessions.

Bottom line

If you want free and open source and uniform stretching is fine → Ultrawidify.
