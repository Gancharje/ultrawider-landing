What it does

Built for ultrawide monitors. Tuned by hand.

Content-aware S-curve

The algorithm stretches the edges of the frame and leaves the center almost untouched. Faces, text and UI in the middle of the shot stay natural — only the outer thirds get warped to fill 21:9.

Adjustable depth

A single slider controls how aggressive the warp is. Crank it for full 21:9 fill, or dial it back for a subtle widescreen feel. Your setting is remembered between sessions.

Works with any YouTube video

Standard videos, Shorts, embedded players, fullscreen, theater mode — all supported. No setup per channel or per video. Toggle once and forget.

Which monitors are supported?

Any ultrawide display: 21:9 (2560×1080, 3440×1440), 32:9 super-ultrawide (5120×1440), or anything wider than standard 16:9. The extension auto-detects your viewport and warps accordingly.

Does it work in all browsers?

Chrome 110+ and any Chromium-based browser of the same vintage (Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi, Arc). Firefox and Safari are not currently supported.

Yes. The free version on the Chrome Web Store gives you basic 21:9 functionality on YouTube. Alpha Access unlocks the content-aware S-curve, the adjustable depth slider, settings persistence, and all future Pro features.
