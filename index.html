What is S-curve warping?

Naive stretching makes everyone look short and wide. Ultrawider applies a content-aware S-curve: pixels at the edges of the frame are warped more aggressively, while the center — where faces, UI elements, and on-screen text usually sit — is left almost untouched. The result: a true 21:9 fill where the subject still looks like a real person, not a melted action figure.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Will it work on Netflix, Disney+ and other DRM services?

Yes — with limitations. DRM-protected streams cannot be processed pixel-by-pixel by extensions, so for services like Netflix and Disney+ Ultrawider falls back to a basic CSS stretch. You get a full-screen 21:9 image, but without the content-aware S-curve. Full warping works on YouTube (Free + Pro) and any other site with non-DRM video (Pro).

Does it reduce video quality?

No. Ultrawider operates as a real-time visual filter on top of the original video stream. Source resolution and bitrate are untouched — we just remap the geometry to fill your ultrawide display.

Which monitors are supported?

Any ultrawide display: 21:9 (2560×1080, 3440×1440), 32:9 super-ultrawide (5120×1440), or anything wider than standard 16:9. The extension auto-detects your viewport and warps accordingly.
