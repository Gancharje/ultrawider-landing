The shader

Quintic smootherstep. No black bars. No flat faces.

The fragment shader remaps every horizontal pixel by blending the identity line with a quintic smootherstep curve. One uniform, u_curveStrength, controls the blend.

f(x) = mix(x, 6x⁵ − 15x⁴ + 10x³, strength)

f(0) = 0, f(1) = 1 — endpoint preservation guarantees no crop. C² continuity at the edges means the warp tapers smoothly into the unstretched boundary. Faces in the centre stay round; edges absorb the stretch.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free unlocks the Smoothie warp on YouTube — that's enough for most casual viewers to fall in love with the feature. Pro from $2.99/month removes the platform gate: Smoothie works on Twitch, Vimeo, RuTube and any other site whose video isn't DRM-protected. See the pricing page for the full comparison.

Which monitors are supported?

Any ultrawide: 21:9 (2560×1080, 3440×1440), 24:9 (3840×1600), 32:9 super-ultrawide (5120×1440), or anything wider than 16:9. The extension auto-detects your viewport aspect and tunes the curve depth so the warp stays in the sweet spot regardless of display.

Does it work on Netflix, HBO, Disney+?

DRM-protected video (Netflix, HBO Max, Disney+, Prime Video) blocks the pixel access Smoothie needs. We can't legitimately warp content we can't read. Pro still unlocks platform-level features there (display tuning, OLED protection — coming soon), but the geometric warp itself is fundamentally blocked by DRM. YouTube, Twitch, Vimeo, RuTube and most independent video hosts work perfectly.

What if I'm not satisfied?

14-day refund window, no questions asked. Email hello@ultrawider.net with the subject "Refund Request" and your order ID. We process refunds within 7–14 business days back to the original payment method. See our refund policy.

Does it work in all browsers?

Chrome 110+ and any Chromium-based browser of the same vintage (Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi, Arc). Firefox and Safari are not currently supported for the extension; the live playground on this page works in any modern browser with WebGL2.
