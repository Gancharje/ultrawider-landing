What it does

Built for ultrawide monitors. Tuned by hand.

Content-aware quintic curve

FAQ

Questions, answered

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free unlocks the Smoothie warp on YouTube only — that's enough for most casual viewers to fall in love with the feature. Pro from $2.99/month removes the platform gate: Smoothie works on Twitch, Vimeo, RuTube and any other site whose video isn't DRM-protected. See the pricing page for the full plan comparison.

Which monitors are supported?

Any ultrawide display: 21:9 (2560×1080, 3440×1440), 24:9 (3840×1600), 32:9 super-ultrawide (5120×1440), or anything wider than standard 16:9. The extension auto-detects your viewport aspect and tunes the curve depth so the warp stays in the sweet spot regardless of display.

Does it work on Netflix, HBO, Disney+?

DRM-protected video (Netflix, HBO Max, Disney+, Prime Video) blocks the kind of pixel access Smoothie needs. We can't legitimately warp content we can't read. Pro still unlocks platform-level features there (display tuning, OLED protection — coming soon), but the geometric warp itself is fundamentally blocked by DRM. YouTube, Twitch, Vimeo, RuTube and most independent video hosts work perfectly.

What if I'm not satisfied?

14-day refund window, no questions asked. Email hello@ultrawider.net with the subject "Refund Request" and your order ID. We process refunds within 7–14 business days back to the original payment method. See our refund policy.

Does it work in all browsers?

Chrome 110+ and any Chromium-based browser of the same vintage (Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi, Arc). Firefox and Safari are not currently supported for the extension itself; this landing page's live playground works in any modern browser that supports WebGL2.
